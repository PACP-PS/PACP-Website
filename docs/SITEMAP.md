## Cross-cutting requirements
- Every public content module supports Arabic and English with independent reviewed text.
- No invented Board members, committee members, events, statistics, testimonials or partners.
- Admin-managed sections may remain unpublished until verified content is supplied.
- Public directory profiles require explicit opt-in.
- No clinical patient records or patient-identifiable health information are collected by this association platform.
- All administrative actions involving member status, finances, notices and permissions are auditable.
