Joining the Association

Learn about the membership procedure, the application requirements and how applications are reviewed under the bylaws and approved regulations.

Membership application

Submit your professional details, contact information and the documents required for review.

Application review

The competent committee checks that the conditions are met and requests further information where needed.

Membership approval

The applicant is notified of the decision, and membership services are activated once the approved requirements are completed.

Proposed membership fee: 100 NIS. Fees and their effective date are recorded once the approval decision is documented. Voting, candidacy, election and renewal rights are to be set after the association's deliberations and approval.
Would you like to join?

You can contact the association to ask about the membership conditions and required documents.

How membership works

1. Submit the application

Enter your details and attach the documents required for your membership category.

2. Application review

The bylaw conditions are checked and the application is referred to the competent body.

3. Decision and notification

The applicant is informed of the decision and next steps, and pays the fee after approval.

Online application is now available after creating an account. Payment is made in cash, by deposit or by transfer; the member uploads a photo of the receipt in the portal for the treasurer to verify.

Online membership services

After creating an account and confirming your email you can submit your application, upload your payment receipt and follow your application's status. Your membership card appears in your portal once the application is approved.

Membership fee payment options

Membership and renewal fees can be paid in two ways, with no online payment gateway. Completing this form is not proof of payment, and membership is activated only after the treasurer's review and approval by the authorised officer.

Choose your preferred payment method

Cash payment: hand the amount to the authorised finance officer; the receipt details are kept for reconciliation.

Upload your payment receipt in the membership portal after submitting your application. Membership is activated only after the treasurer's verification and approval by the authorised officer.
